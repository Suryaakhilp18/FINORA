### Slide 7: The Deterministic Math Guardrail
* **Slide Title**: Why FINORA Never Hallucinates Numbers
* **Visuals**:
  * Dual-Engine Architecture Diagram:
    * `Deterministic Financial Engine (Python)` ➔ Math, EMI, Runway, GST, Runway Months.
    * `Cognitive AI Layer (Gemini LLM)` ➔ Tone, Nudges, Psychological Explanation, Context.
* **Slide Bullet Points**:
  * **The Fatal Flaw of Financial AI**: Pure LLMs hallucinate compounding interest, amortization, and dates.
  * **FINORA's Guardrail Separation**:
    * **100% Deterministic Python Core**: Standard banking formulas (Reducing-balance EMI, 18% GST on interest, runway depletion days).
    * **Gemini AI Intelligence Layer**: Interprets user intent, extracts shopping cart data, analyzes psychology, and delivers empathetic coaching.
  * **Bank-Grade Reliability**: Every single rupee on the dashboard is backed by audited mathematical functions.
* **🎤 Speaker Script**:
  > *"A critical engineering decision we made: We NEVER allow the Large Language Model to calculate financial figures. Pure LLMs hallucinate interest rates and compound arithmetic. Instead, FINORA uses a strict separation of concerns: 100% of the numbers, EMIs, GST calculations, and runways are computed by a deterministic Python financial engine. Gemini AI provides natural language understanding, behavioral coaching, and semantic reasoning. It is mathematically infallible and psychologically smart."*
